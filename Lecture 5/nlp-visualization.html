Metric Definitions

Accuracy = (TP + TN) / Total — overall correctness.

Precision = TP / (TP + FP) — of predicted positives, how many are real.

Recall = TP / (TP + FN) — of real positives, how many did we catch.

F1 = 2 × Precision × Recall / (Precision + Recall) — harmonic mean.

High Precision, Low Recall: cautious classifier — few false alarms but misses many positives (spam filter).

Low Precision, High Recall: aggressive classifier — catches most positives but noisy (medical screening).

F1 balances both — best when classes are imbalanced.
