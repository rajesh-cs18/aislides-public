Machine Learning: Interactive Algorithm Demos

Classroom-friendly visualizations for Linear Regression, k-NN, Decision Tree inference, and k-Means clustering.

Linear Regression

Move slope/intercept manually, then compare with auto-fit.

Blue points are training data. Orange line is your current model; dashed green line is the hidden true trend.

k-NN

Click on the plot to place a query point. The nearest neighbors vote.

Blue and orange are known classes. Dashed links show the k nearest points used for the vote.

Decision Tree Inference

Pick feature values and watch the path from root to leaf.

Highlighted branches show exactly why the model predicts Play or No.

k-Means Clustering

Initialize centroids, then iterate assign/update steps.

Points are colored by assigned cluster; X markers are centroids. WCSS decreases as clusters stabilize.
