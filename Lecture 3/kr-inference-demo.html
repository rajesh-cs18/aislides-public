How to Read Result

Variables are lowercase (e.g., x, y).

Constants and predicate names are treated as symbols.

Unification fails when predicate names differ, arity differs, or constants conflict.

Example failure:
Treats(DrSmith,Sam)
Treats(DrJones,Sam)
Constants DrSmith and DrJones conflict.
